Stairio V7
PLA casing thermal screening

Conditional wall-only results—not operating temperatures of the robot. Each case puts 1 W directly into selected inner casing walls. It excludes heat carried out through vents, actual fan airflow, electronics, motor contacts, lid contacts and radiation. Do not multiply these results by total system electrical power or use them as a pass/fail decision for PLA.

Geometry successfully imported

The July SolidWorks archive imported as 11 solids for the housing and 235 solids from STAIRIO.SLDASM. The subsequent assembly audit found that the main shell is absent from the 235-body import; the shell used in this study comes from the separate housing import. See the newer ventilation report for the geometry audit. The primary housing spans approximately 72 × 123 × 120 mm. Its major capsule sidewalls have 31 mm inner and 33 mm outer radii/half-widths: approximately 2 mm wall thickness. The STEP-translated primary volume is 68.90 cm³, about 0.11% above the 68.83 cm³ Discovery import. This small translation difference was recorded.

The confirmed installed motor/controller is M2006 P36/C610. The archive contains a differently named 36GY-3536 motor part; no automatic motor replacement or assumed component placement was made.

The user confirms the installed, continuously running fan is fan-4007. The historical V7 assembly record identifies housing-v7-2/fan-4007-1 with a local CAD envelope of 40 × 7 × 42 mm and a recorded placement transform. Manufacturer, supply voltage, airflow direction and pressure-flow curve remain unverified. No fan performance has been added to the shell-only results below.

Exact model definition

  • Heated upper inner sidewalls: faces 3414, 2919, 3004, 3005, 2920; total area 0.0213920 m². A single total 1 W heat-flow condition is distributed over this area.
  • Exterior convection: faces 3415, 3009, 3011, 3010, 2938; total CAD area 0.0246259 m².
  • All other primary-shell surfaces are adiabatic. Other ten housing solids are suppressed. There is no fluid domain, internal convection model, radiation or component contact model.
  • PLA inputs: k=0.2 W/(m·K), density 1240 kg/m³, cp=1800 J/(kg·K), all provisional. Density and cp do not affect this steady-state result. Printed infill and anisotropy are not represented.

Verification and limits

All four Mechanical cases reported Done, with zero solver errors. Each solve logged two warnings: shape checking disabled because Workbench meshing performs its checks, and a material-energy warning for the surface-effect element material. The volumetric PLA has its thermal conductivity explicitly assigned. These warnings are preserved in each project's solver files.

Independent integration of the exported solver loads and nodal temperatures: PASS. The worst heat-balance residual across all four cases is 0.0001203% of the applied watt. This checks the numerical solution of the specified model, not its physical assumptions. Detailed evidence is saved in shell-screen-verification.json.

For scale, the Pi manufacturer's 350 mA typical bare-board active current corresponds to about 1.75 W at 5 V. It is not this robot's measured dissipation. Motor CAN torque current is not DC-bus current. Power-converter output power must not be counted a second time as converter heat.

PLA softening needs attention: an example Prusament grade reports 55°C heat-deflection temperature. This is neither the installed filament's limit nor a permissible continuous service temperature.
